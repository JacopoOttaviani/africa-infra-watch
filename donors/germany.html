The ministry's own records: GIZ, KfW and the rest

The Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ) publishes every project it pays for to IATI, one file per partner country and per region, and names in each the agency that carries it out. The site reads the __BMZ_FILES__ African files: __BMZ_N__ projects, EUR __BMZ_EUR_BN__ bn committed since __BMZ_FROM__, __BMZ_PAID_PCT__% of it paid out; __BMZ_RUNNING__ are running now. By money __BMZ_TOP_GROUP__ comes first, EUR __BMZ_TOP_GROUP_BN__ bn, then __BMZ_TOP2_GROUP__, EUR __BMZ_TOP2_GROUP_BN__ bn. __BMZ_TOP_COUNTRY__ has received the most, EUR __BMZ_TOP_COUNTRY_BN__ bn over __BMZ_TOP_COUNTRY_N__ projects, then __BMZ_TOP2_COUNTRY__ and __BMZ_TOP3_COUNTRY__; __BMZ_TOP_ACTIVE_COUNTRY__ has the most running now, __BMZ_TOP_ACTIVE_N__.

Three channels carry the money. KfW, the development bank, does financial cooperation, the loans and grants that pay for plants, networks and credit lines: __BMZ_KFW_N__ projects, EUR __BMZ_KFW_EUR_BN__ bn, __BMZ_KFW_RUNNING__ running. GIZ, the federal agency for international cooperation, does technical cooperation, advisers and training beside the partner ministries: __BMZ_GIZ_N__ projects, EUR __BMZ_GIZ_EUR_BN__ bn, __BMZ_GIZ_RUNNING__ running. The other __BMZ_OTHER_N__, EUR __BMZ_OTHER_EUR_BN__ bn, go through German NGOs and church agencies, foundations, Engagement Global and UN agencies such as the World Food Programme. __BMZ_REGIONAL_N__ regional programmes, EUR __BMZ_REGIONAL_EUR_BN__ bn, name a region and no country; they are in the totals, not in the country table or on the maps.

BMZ places every project at the partner country's capital, described as the place of the agreement with the government, never at a site: these records say where Germany works, not where it builds. They are the ministry's own; the CRS below is Germany's whole report to the OECD, BMZ's money among it, and the two are never added.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

Germany reports to the OECD at activity level, with __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records and descriptions that name the partner institution and often the region. The ministry's bilateral programmes are the bulk; the loans are KfW's, to Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Kenya and the other economies it lends to on its own terms with a federal subsidy; the research ministry's many small records are university partnerships; DEG's are equity and loans to companies, reported as private-sector instruments.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (__CRS_PLACED__ records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

Country-year series: investment stock, net aid, debt

Three official series describe German money in Africa one country and one year at a time. The OECD's FDI positions, which for Germany are the Bundesbank's figures, give the outward direct-investment stock by partner country. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes German creditors: KfW's development loans and the older claims that survived the debt relief of the 2000s, and loans from German banks.

How to read this page

One record is
In the BMZ tables, one BMZ project as the ministry publishes it, under the agency that carries it out; a triangle on the profile map is a country's projects together. In the aid tables, one CRS activity as Germany reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, as the map carries it. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
BMZ projects carry IATI's status: 2 is running now (under construction on the main map), 3 and 4 finished. GEM records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, all resident units, immediate counterpart. Debt is the World Bank's public and publicly guaranteed stock owed to the lender's creditors, the state and its banks together, as the debt page counts it, with the bilateral official part, the state's own claims, beside it. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders. BMZ's commitments are in euros as it publishes them, from IATI commitment transactions; paid out is KfW's disbursements and GIZ's and the other channels' expenditure. The main map converts them to dollars at its stated rate and counts only the infrastructure share; neither is added to the CRS dollars.
Location
Aid, BMZ's projects and the three series are drawn at the country's interior point; BMZ gives every project the capital's point, the place of the agreement. Power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • GIZ's own IATI file holds only the work other clients commission from it (the European Commission, the UK, the Netherlands), with no locations; GIZ's work for BMZ is in BMZ's files, read above. BMZ publishes no country file for Comoros, Equatorial Guinea, Mauritius or Seychelles, and gives no project a site.
  • DEG's portfolio is published as a list of companies and countries without amounts per investment; Hermes export-credit guarantees have no project register.
  • The federal government's Compact with Africa and its Africa strategy are policy documents, not datasets.
