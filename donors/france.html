Where it is on record

Aid is drawn at each country's interior point, sized by seven years of commitments; the CRS carries no coordinates, so a country point is the honest place. AFD's 150 largest infrastructure financings are crosses, where the main map draws them, in its status colours. TotalEnergies' pipelines are drawn as routes, Orange's cables as dotted lines, power units in the map's status colours. Debt owed to France is off until switched on.

The pipelines, power units and cables are the map's own records with a French company among the owners, TotalEnergies' share of a Nigerian or Mozambican line included; the profile does not add records, it filters. Routes that leave the continent are cut at the frame.

AFD's own records: the map's crosses

The Agence française de développement publishes its operations to IATI, one file per country: the project, and under it the financings, each a loan or a grant with its own commitment and disbursements. The map reads the __AFD_FILES__ African files and keeps the infrastructure financings by the same DAC codes as the African Development Bank and the World Bank: __AFD_N__ of them, EUR __AFD_EUR_BN__ bn committed (USD __AFD_USD_BN__ bn), __AFD_DISB_PCT__% of it paid out so far. Loans are __AFD_LOANS_N__ of the records and USD __AFD_LOANS_USD_BN__ bn; the __AFD_GRANTS_N__ grants add USD __AFD_GRANTS_USD_BN__ bn. By money __AFD_TOP_SECTOR__ comes first, USD __AFD_TOP_SECTOR_BN__ bn, then __AFD_TOP2_SECTOR__, USD __AFD_TOP2_SECTOR_BN__ bn; __AFD_TOP_COUNTRY__ receives the most, USD __AFD_TOP_COUNTRY_USD__ m over __AFD_TOP_COUNTRY_N__ financings.

AFD's files hold signed financings only: __AFD_BUILDING_N__ under implementation, __AFD_DONE_N__ completed, none announced or cancelled. The agency files its policy loans as project aid, so the map leaves out budget support by AFD's own words ("financement budgétaire", "prêt de politique publique"): __AFD_BUDGET__ financings. Each financing carries one unnamed point and no exactness; in every country file one point holds most of them, the agency's office (Dakar serves Senegal, Gambia and Guinea-Bissau), so the __AFD_AT_OFFICE__ financings placed there are drawn at the country's centre and the other __AFD_GIVEN__ where AFD puts them. These are AFD's own records; the CRS below is France's whole report to the OECD, the AFD's loans among them, and the two are never added.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

France reports to the OECD at activity level, with __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records. The agency's loans to governments and public utilities are the large lines; the foreign ministry's scholarships, the research institutes and the local authorities' decentralised cooperation make up the count. Debt treatments under the Paris Club appear as debt-relief records in the years they are granted.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (__CRS_PLACED__ records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

Country-year series: investment stock, net aid, debt

Three official series describe French money in Africa one country and one year at a time. The OECD's FDI positions, the Banque de France's figures, give the outward direct-investment stock by partner country: Morocco, then the oil economies and South Africa. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes French creditors: the agency's loans and the old claims the Paris Club has not yet treated, and loans from French banks.

How to read this page

One record is
In the AFD table and the crosses, one AFD financing, a loan or a grant under a project, as the map carries it. In the Expertise France table, one European Commission contract from the map's EU layer. In the aid tables, one CRS activity as France reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
AFD financings, GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping: IATI status 2 is under construction, 3 and 4 operating. Aid activities have no status.
Money
AFD's commitments are in euros as it publishes them, converted to dollars at the map's stated rate, the infrastructure share of each financing by its own sector percentages. CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, all resident units, immediate counterpart. Debt is the World Bank's public and publicly guaranteed stock owed to the lender's creditors, the state and its banks together, as the debt page counts it, with the bilateral official part, the state's own claims, beside it. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
AFD financings are drawn where the main map draws them: the point AFD gives, or the country's interior point where that point is the agency's office. Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • Proparco, AFD's private-sector arm, has no publisher entry on the IATI Registry, so its loans to companies are not here; AFD's multi-country file, which mixes African and other recipients, is left out. The crosses are AFD's country files only, infrastructure only.
  • Bpifrance export credits have no project register. Expertise France publishes no project file of its own; the EU contracts it carries out are listed above, from the map's EU layer. The decentralised cooperation of the regions appears only as CRS records.
  • Oil and gas fields with French operators are in GEM's extraction tracker, released through a request form only; the pipelines are the open part.
