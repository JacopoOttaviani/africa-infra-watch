Where it is on record

Aid is drawn at each country's interior point, sized by seven years of commitments; the CRS carries no coordinates, so a country point is the honest place. TotalEnergies' pipelines are drawn as routes, Orange's cables as dotted lines, power units in the map's status colours. Debt owed to France is off until switched on.

The pipelines, power units and cables are the map's own records with a French company among the owners, TotalEnergies' share of a Nigerian or Mozambican line included; the profile does not add records, it filters. Routes that leave the continent are cut at the frame.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

France reports to the OECD at activity level, with __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records. The agency's loans to governments and public utilities are the large lines; the foreign ministry's scholarships, the research institutes and the local authorities' decentralised cooperation make up the count. Debt treatments under the Paris Club appear as debt-relief records in the years they are granted.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (__CRS_PLACED__ records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

Country-year series: investment stock, net aid, debt

Three official series describe French money in Africa one country and one year at a time. The OECD's FDI positions, the Banque de France's figures, give the outward direct-investment stock by partner country: Morocco, then the oil economies and South Africa. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes France, the agency's loans and the old claims the Paris Club has not yet treated.

How to read this page

One record is
In the aid tables, one CRS activity as France reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, all resident units, immediate counterpart. Debt is the World Bank's bilateral public and publicly guaranteed stock. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • The AFD's and Proparco's IATI files carry locations and documents for the agency's projects; the site does not read them yet. They would put French aid on the project map.
  • Bpifrance export credits have no project register; Expertise France and the decentralised cooperation of the regions appear only as CRS records.
  • Oil and gas fields with French operators are in GEM's extraction tracker, released through a request form only; the pipelines are the open part.
