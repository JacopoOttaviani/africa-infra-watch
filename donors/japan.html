Japan

Japan is the lender that builds: JICA's yen loans for ports, roads, power and water are the spine of its record. __CRS_N__ activities with an African recipient for __CRS_FROM__–__CRS_TO__, USD __CRS_USD_BN__ bn committed, __CRS_LOANS_N__ of them loans; __CRS_AGENCY_TOP_PCT__% of the activities are reported by the __CRS_AGENCY_TOP__. The largest purpose is __CRS_TOP_SECTOR__, USD __CRS_TOP_SECTOR_USD__ m; __CRS_TOP_COUNTRY__, __CRS_TOP2_COUNTRY__ and __CRS_TOP3_COUNTRY__ receive the most. __CRS_HARD_N__ records, USD __CRS_HARD_USD__ m, build, renovate or drill something, a larger share than for any other provider on this site.

The corporate footprint is thinner than the lending. Japan reports its direct-investment stock in Africa for South Africa alone, USD __FDI_STOCK_BN__ bn at the end of __FDI_TO__; the map's own layers carry __N_GEM__ power units owned by Eurus Energy, Toyota Tsusho or Sumitomo, wind and solar in Egypt, Kenya and South Africa, and __N_PIPE_SEG__ pipeline segment with Sojitz among the owners. Where it is on record

Aid is drawn at each country's interior point, sized by seven years of commitments; the CRS carries no coordinates, so a country point is the honest place. Power units with a Japanese owner sit at Global Energy Monitor's coordinates in the map's status colours. Debt owed to Japan is off until switched on.

JICA's own project map and its IATI files place the yen-loan projects; the site does not read them yet, so the loans sit at country points here and the works they paid for appear on the main map only where another source holds them.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

Japan reports to the OECD at activity level, with __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records: JICA's loans, grants and technical cooperation, the foreign ministry's grassroots grants, the ministries' and universities' smaller lines. The loans are the money: a port in Mombasa or Nacala, a bridge, a thermal or geothermal plant, a water system, each a single record of several hundred million dollars with the project named in the title. The grants are the count.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (__CRS_PLACED__ records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

Country-year series: net aid, debt and what Japan reports of investment

Two official series describe Japanese money in Africa one country and one year at a time, and a third only in part. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes Japan, which is the yen-loan portfolio country by country. The OECD's FDI positions carry a Japanese figure for South Africa only; Japan's own statistics publish Africa as one aggregate.

How to read this page

One record is
In the aid tables, one CRS activity as Japan reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, immediate counterpart, where Japan reports it. Debt is the World Bank's bilateral public and publicly guaranteed stock. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • JICA's IATI files and project map carry locations for the yen-loan and grant projects; the site does not read them yet. They would put Japanese aid on the project map more precisely than any other provider's.
  • JBIC's and NEXI's export finance to private borrowers has no project register.
  • Japan's FDI by African country: the Bank of Japan and JETRO publish Africa as one aggregate; the OECD holds a figure for South Africa only.
  • Submarine cables: the map's cable layer names no Japanese owner on an African landing.
