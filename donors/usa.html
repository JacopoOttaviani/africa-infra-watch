United States

The United States is the largest single provider of aid to Africa in the OECD's record, and only a small part of it is infrastructure. __CRS_N__ activities with an African recipient for __CRS_FROM__–__CRS_TO__, USD __CRS_USD_BN__ bn committed, __CRS_GRANT_PCT__% of them grants; __CRS_AGENCY_TOP_PCT__% are reported by the __CRS_AGENCY_TOP__, and the largest purpose by far is __CRS_TOP_SECTOR__, USD __CRS_TOP_SECTOR_USD__ m. Only __CRS_HARD_N__ records, USD __CRS_HARD_USD__ m, build, renovate or drill something. The 2024 figures are the last full year before the agency was folded into the State Department in 2025.

The money that does build is private and corporate: US direct-investment stock in Africa stood at USD __FDI_STOCK_BN__ bn at the end of __FDI_TO__, __FDI_TOP__, __FDI_TOP2__ and __FDI_TOP3__ first, and the map's own layers carry __N_PIPES__ pipelines with Chevron, ExxonMobil, ConocoPhillips, Apache or Occidental among the owners, __N_GEM__ power units with a US owner, and __N_CABLES__ submarine cables owned by Google, Meta or AT&T. What African governments owe the United States is USD __IDS_STOCK__ m at the end of __IDS_TO__, what is left after the debt relief of the 2000s.

Where it is on record

Aid is drawn at each country's interior point, sized by seven years of commitments; the CRS names no coordinates, so a country point is the honest place. The oil majors' pipelines are drawn as routes, the platforms' cables as dotted lines, power units in the map's status colours. Debt owed to the United States is off until switched on.

The pipelines, power units and cables are the map's own records with a US company among the owners, Chevron's share of the West African Gas Pipeline included; the profile does not add records, it filters. Routes that leave the continent, Google's Equiano to Portugal and Meta's lines to Europe and Asia, are cut at the frame.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

The United States reports to the OECD at activity level, with __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records: USAID's programmes, the State Department's, PEPFAR through Health and Human Services, the Peace Corps, the Millennium Challenge Corporation's compacts, the African Development Foundation's grants. The record is health, humanitarian relief and governance; the compacts and a handful of energy and water programmes are the infrastructure, and the development finance corporation's loans and guarantees appear under private-sector instruments where they count as official flows.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions name implementing partners more often than places (__CRS_PLACED__ records mention a town), and the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

Pipelines, power units and cables with a US owner

The map's energy and connectivity layers carry an owner field; this table is those records filtered for a US company. The pipelines are the oil majors' export and field lines in Egypt, Libya, Nigeria and the Gulf of Guinea, with Chevron's stake in the West African Gas Pipeline; the power units include General Electric's and Symbion's stakes; the cables are Google's Equiano down the Atlantic coast and Meta's share of 2Africa, with AT&T in the older consortia.

Country-year series: investment stock, net aid, debt

Three official series describe US money in Africa one country and one year at a time. The OECD's FDI positions, which for the United States are the Bureau of Economic Analysis's figures, give the outward direct-investment stock by partner country; Mauritius's place near the top is a holding-company address, not plants. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes the United States, mostly old food-aid and Export-Import Bank claims that survived the debt relief of the 2000s.

How to read this page

One record is
In the aid tables, one CRS activity as the United States reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, all resident units, immediate counterpart. Debt is the World Bank's bilateral public and publicly guaranteed stock. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
Aid and the three series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • The development finance corporation's active projects are a web table updated quarterly, with no download; its IATI files are near-empty.
  • The Millennium Challenge Corporation publishes its compacts' data on its own portal; the compacts appear here only as CRS records.
  • ForeignAssistance.gov carries the same activities as the CRS with more detail and a bulk download; the OECD record is used so that every provider on the site is read the same way.
  • Power Africa's transaction tracker is a programme list, not a register of owners, and is not read.
  • Oil and gas fields with US operators are in GEM's extraction tracker, released through a request form only.
