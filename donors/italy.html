Italy

Italy is the one lender on this site with an official, placed project list. The Piano Mattei portal names __MATTEI_N__ projects in __MATTEI_COUNTRIES__ countries, with a stage, an implementer and a stated figure for __MATTEI_WITH_AMOUNT__ of them, EUR __MATTEI_EUR_BN__ bn in all; the map already draws them as hexagons. Behind the plan sits a full aid record, reported to the OECD at activity level with real titles: __CRS_N__ Italian activities with an African recipient for __CRS_FROM__–__CRS_TO__, USD __CRS_USD_BN__ bn committed, most of them not the government's own but the projects of religious bodies, NGOs and universities funded through Italy's tax-designation schemes. The development agency's own files add the detail for its share: __AICS_N__ activities, EUR __AICS_EUR_BN__ bn committed.

The money that dwarfs both is private. Italian direct-investment stock in Africa stood at USD __FDI_STOCK_BN__ bn at the end of __FDI_TO__, __FDI_TOP__ and __FDI_TOP2__ first, where Eni produces gas and oil; the map's own layers carry __N_PIPES__ pipelines with Eni, Snam or Edison among the owners, __N_GEM__ power units owned by Enel, Eni, Building Energy or Renco, and __N_CABLES__ submarine cables with Sparkle. What African governments owe Italian creditors is small by comparison: USD __IDS_STOCK__ m at the end of __IDS_TO__, USD __IDS_BL_STOCK__ m of it to the Italian state.

Where it is on record

Piano Mattei projects sit where the map puts them: a named site or town where the portal's description gives one, otherwise the country's interior point, and one fixed point in the Central African Republic for programmes across several countries. Aid is drawn at each country's interior point, sized by seven years of commitments. Eni's pipelines are drawn as routes, Sparkle's cables as dotted lines, power units in the map's status colours. Debt owed to Italy is off until switched on.

The pipelines, power units and cables are the map's own records with an Italian name among the owners, Eni's share of a Nigerian or Libyan line included; the profile does not add records, it filters. Routes that leave the continent, the Trans-Mediterranean and Greenstream lines into Italy and the cables to the Gulf and India, are cut at the frame.

Piano Mattei: the Government's own list

The Presidency of the Council publishes one page per project, with the plan's directives, the objective, the countries, the implementing body, the partners, the funding source and a five-step progress bar. The map reads all of it and states, on every card, how precisely the project could be placed. __MATTEI_TOP_SECTOR_N__ of the __MATTEI_N__ projects are __MATTEI_TOP_SECTOR_BY_N__; by stated money the largest sector is __MATTEI_TOP_SECTOR__. Stages: __MATTEI_STAGES__.

The figures are the portal's own and are read as such: a stated amount may be the whole project's value, other funders' shares included, or Italy's contribution, and the portal does not say which. ELMED, the Tunisia–Sicily interconnector, carries EUR 1.4 bn that is mostly European and Tunisian money; a Climate Fund loan is Italy's alone. The amounts are therefore shown on a line of their own and never added to the lenders' commitments, here or on the map.

The development agency's own record: AICS OpenAid

The Italian Agency for Development Cooperation (AICS) publishes every initiative it manages to the International Aid Transparency Initiative, in the files behind its OpenAid portal: __AICS_N__ activities with an African recipient, EUR __AICS_EUR_BN__ bn committed and EUR __AICS_DISB_BN__ bn paid out, net of revisions. __AICS_RUNNING__ are in implementation, __AICS_FINALISING__ in finalisation, __AICS_CLOSED__ closed and __AICS_CANCELLED__ cancelled. They reach __AICS_COUNTRIES__ countries, __AICS_TOP3__ first, plus __AICS_REGIONAL_N__ regional programmes worth EUR __AICS_REGIONAL_EUR__ m.

Every record is a grant. The agency runs __AICS_AGENCY_N__ of them itself, through its field offices; __AICS_NGO_N__ go through Italian and local NGOs; UN agencies and other multilaterals take __AICS_MULTI_PCT__% of the money. Commitments reached EUR __AICS_PEAK_EUR__ m in __AICS_PEAK_YEAR__, against an average of EUR __AICS_PRE_AVG__ m a year over __AICS_PRE_FROM__–__AICS_PRE_TO__. The largest new lines are the agri-food programmes of CIHEAM, the Mediterranean agronomic institute in Bari: __AICS_CIHEAM_N__ activities, EUR __AICS_CIHEAM_EUR__ m. Three of them, in Ghana, Senegal and the Republic of the Congo, are the Piano Mattei's AREA Africa programme, and their commitments add up to the figure the plan's portal states for it: the same money, listed once by the plan and once by the agency that pays it out.

These are the projects the OECD's Creditor Reporting System carries under the agency's name (__CRS_AICS_N__ CRS records over __CRS_FROM__–__CRS_TO__, in the next section), seen from the other end: one record per funding line for its whole life, in euros, with the implementing partner, the status and a page on the portal, where the CRS has one line per year in dollars. The euros are therefore never added to the CRS dollars, here or anywhere else on the site. The files do not place the projects: __AICS_LOCATED__ activities carry a location, and in each country it is the same unnamed point, the agency's field office in the capital. The profile map leaves them off, as it does the CRS records.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

Italy reports its aid to the OECD at activity level, with __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records over the seven years, descriptions that name partners and often towns, a purpose code, a channel and a finance type. What makes the record unusual is who reports it: __CRS_AGENCY_TOP_PCT__% of the activities come under "__CRS_AGENCY_TOP__", the tax-designation scheme through which taxpayers assign a share of their income tax, and a further large share from the NGO scheme and from public universities. The Agency for Development Cooperation, the regions and the foreign ministry report fewer, larger records. The biggest single lines are not projects at all: the 2021 cancellation of Somalia's debt under the HIPC initiative, the policy-based loans to Kenya and Rwanda, and the Italian Climate Fund's bond and equity subscriptions.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a town or a district (__CRS_PLACED__ of the records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates and no gazetteer could be trusted at this scale, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map, as for the other lenders. The agency's own IATI files, in the section above, do not place it either.

Pipelines, power units and cables with an Italian owner

The map's energy and connectivity layers carry an owner field; this table is those records filtered for an Italian name. Eni's position is the largest of any Italian company on the continent: the Trans-Mediterranean line from Algeria through Tunisia, Greenstream from Libya, the Wafa–Mellitah and Sharara lines in Libya, the Zohr and Damietta lines in Egypt, the Brass and Tebidaba oil network in the Niger Delta, and a solar plant with Sonatrach at Bir Rebaa. Enel Green Power owns __GEM_MW__ MW of operating wind and solar, most of it in South Africa. Sparkle, Telecom Italia's international arm, sits in the ownership of __N_CABLES__ cables that land in Africa.

Country-year series: investment stock, net aid, debt

Three official series describe Italian money in Africa one country and one year at a time. The OECD's FDI positions give Italy's outward direct-investment stock by partner country, which is where the oil and gas is: __FDI_TOP__, __FDI_TOP2__ and __FDI_TOP3__ lead. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes Italian creditors, the state and its banks together; the state's part is what remains of decades of bilateral lending after the debt relief of the 2000s.

How to read this page

One record is
In the Piano Mattei table, one project page on the Government's portal. In the agency tables, one AICS activity as the agency publishes it to IATI, a funding line for its whole life (a second tranche of the same initiative is a record of its own). In the aid tables, one CRS activity as Italy reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
Piano Mattei stages map as on the map: identified and formulated are announced, approved is approved, ongoing is under construction, completed is operating. GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. AICS activities keep the IATI word (implementation, finalisation, closed, cancelled), coloured as the map colours IATI records. CRS activities have no status.
Money
Piano Mattei amounts are the portal's figures in euros, shown as stated. AICS commitments and disbursements are in euros, the sum of the files' transactions net of revisions (about one transaction in eight is negative). CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, all resident units, immediate counterpart. Debt is the World Bank's public and publicly guaranteed stock owed to the lender's creditors, the state and its banks together, as the debt page counts it, with the bilateral official part, the state's own claims, beside it. None of these is added to the map's lenders, and none is added to the others.
Location
Piano Mattei projects use the map's placement: a site or town named in the description, a named administrative area, the country's interior point, or the plan's fixed continental point. AICS activities are not drawn: the only location in the files is the agency's field office, one unnamed point per country. Aid and the three series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • Project sites for the agency's work. AICS's IATI files carry no site locations, only the field office; OpenAid's documents and photos, linked from each activity page, can name a town.
  • SACE, the export-credit agency, publishes no register of insured or guaranteed transactions; Cassa Depositi e Prestiti publishes no project list for its lending in Africa. Where either shows up, it is through the CRS loan records or the Piano Mattei pages.
  • Eni's and others' oil and gas fields are in GEM's Oil and Gas Extraction Tracker, released through a request form only.
  • The OECD's bulk CRS files sit behind a browser challenge; the page uses the SDMX microdata query, one year at a time.
