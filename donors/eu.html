European Union

The European Union's own institutions publish their records, and the map already draws them. The European Commission's contracts and financing decisions and the European Investment Bank's operations come from the institutions' IATI files: __LAYER_N__ infrastructure records in the map's EU finance layer, EUR __LAYER_EUR_BN__ bn committed, __LAYER_EC_N__ of them the Commission's and __LAYER_EIB_N__ the Bank's. Behind the layer sits the OECD record of the EU institutions as an aid provider: __CRS_N__ activities with an African recipient for __CRS_FROM__–__CRS_TO__, USD __CRS_USD_BN__ bn committed, __CRS_TOP_COUNTRY__, __CRS_TOP2_COUNTRY__ and __CRS_TOP3_COUNTRY__ first.

What is not here is as telling: the member states' own agencies (AFD, KfW, Enabel, the Italian agency) are national and sit on their own pages, the EFSD+ guarantees that carry Global Gateway have no commitment transaction and leave no record, and the Union reports no investment stock because it is not an investor. The debt side is the Bank's: USD __IDS_EIB_BN__ bn owed to the EIB by __IDS_N__ governments at the end of __IDS_TO__, and USD __IDS_EU_BN__ bn owed to the EU budget and the European Development Fund, almost all of it Tunisia's and Egypt's macro-financial assistance.

Where it is on record

The layer's commitments are summed at each country's interior point and sized; the 150 largest records are drawn as stars where the map places them, in its status colours, with every one of the __LAYER_N__ on the main map. The map's three placement tiers apply: a named place (__LAYER_NAMED__ records), the country's point (__LAYER_COUNTRY__) and the Commission's Africa-wide point in Chad for regional programmes (__LAYER_REGIONAL__). Debt owed to the EIB is off until switched on.

The layer and the aid record overlap by design: the Commission's infrastructure contracts are a subset of what it reports to the OECD, and the EIB's operations are the Bank's own. The layer is what can be placed; the OECD record is everything the institutions spent. The two are shown side by side and never added.

The map's EU finance layer: the institutions' own records

The Commission publishes two levels in one IATI file, financing decisions (the whole envelope) and the contracts under them; the layer keeps one record per contract and a decision only where none of its contracts is in the set, so a pipeline action is one announced record and no euro is counted twice. The EIB publishes one global file with no locations at all, which is why its operations sit at the country's interior point. The same infrastructure filter applies as for the African Development Bank and the World Bank: the DAC codes for transport, energy, communications, water, industry and trade. By money the layer's largest sector is __LAYER_TOP_SECTOR__, USD __LAYER_TOP_SECTOR_BN__ bn; the Commission's part is USD __LAYER_EC_USD_BN__ bn and the Bank's USD __LAYER_EIB_USD_BN__ bn.

Country-year series: net aid and debt

Two official series describe EU money one country and one year at a time. The DAC2a table gives the institutions' net ODA by recipient. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes the European Investment Bank, which lends on its own balance sheet, and, separately, what it owes the EU budget and the European Development Fund, which is the macro-financial assistance loans and the few EDF special loans still outstanding. There is no investment-stock series: the Union is not an investor and the member states' stocks are on their own pages.

How to read this page

One record is
In the layer table, one Commission contract or financing decision, or one EIB operation, as the map carries it. In the aid tables, one CRS activity as the EU institutions report it. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
Layer records use the map's mapping of IATI activity status: 1 announced, 2 under construction, 3 and 4 operating, 5 and 6 stalled; a Commission financing decision still in the pipeline is an announced record. Aid activities have no status.
Money
Layer amounts are the institutions' euros, converted at the rate the map states and never added to the other lenders. CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. Debt is the World Bank's stock in current dollars. None is added to another.
Location
The layer's three tiers, stated on every card: a named place, the country's interior point, or the Commission's Africa-wide point in Chad for regional programmes. Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point.
Not here, and why
  • Global Gateway is a label on existing instruments, not a dataset; its flagship list is a web page without amounts per project.
  • EFSD+ guarantees, the main Global Gateway instrument, have no commitment transaction in the IATI files and leave no record here.
  • The member states' agencies (AFD, KfW, Enabel, AICS and the others) are national providers and sit on their own pages where one exists.
  • Investment stock: the OECD's FDI positions have no reporter for the Union as such.
