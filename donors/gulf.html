Where it is on record

Aid is drawn at each country's interior point, sized by seven years of commitments; the CRS carries no coordinates, so a country point is the honest place. Aramco's and the Gulf companies' pipelines are drawn as routes, the carriers' cables as dotted lines, power units in the map's status colours. Debt owed to the Gulf states is off until switched on.

The four providers are summed in the bubbles; the per-provider split is in the chart below. The power units are the map's own records with a Gulf company among the owners; the profile does not add records, it filters. DP World's and AD Ports' terminals, the other large Gulf footprint, are not in any of the map's layers.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

The four report to the OECD at activity level as non-DAC providers: the Emirates' ministry and its foundations, the Saudi Fund for Development and the King Salman centre, the Kuwait Fund for Arab Economic Development, the Qatar Fund for Development and Qatar Charity. Together they give __CRS_TITLES__ distinct titles for __CRS_N__ records. The loans are the Saudi and Kuwait funds' project lending for roads, dams, hospitals and power; the grants are budget support, humanitarian aid and the Emirates' and Qatar's charities' projects.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (__CRS_PLACED__ records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

Country-year series: net aid and debt

Two official series describe Gulf money in Africa one country and one year at a time, and the second comes twice. The DAC2a table gives net ODA by recipient, summed over the four. The World Bank's debt statistics give what each government owes the four states, and, separately, what it owes the Gulf-based funds, the Arab Fund, the OPEC Fund, the Islamic Development Bank and BADEA, which lend on the same terms from the same capitals. There is no investment-stock series: none of the four reports to the OECD's FDI collection.

How to read this page

One record is
In the aid tables, one CRS activity as each of the four reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. Debt is the World Bank's bilateral public and publicly guaranteed stock. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • Investment stock by African country: none of the four reports to the OECD; the Emirates' and Saudi statistics publish Africa as a region, and the sovereign funds publish no portfolio by country.
  • DP World's and AD Ports' terminals, the largest Gulf footprint on the continent's coasts, are in none of the map's layers; a port layer would be the way to show them.
  • The Arab Coordination Group's project lists are the funds' own websites, one per fund, with no common machine-readable register.
  • Oil and gas fields with Gulf operators are in GEM's extraction tracker, released through a request form only.
