## Notes

- **No drift.** The 14 Oct downloads reproduce every site figure exactly, so each WRONG is a rule problem, not stale data.
- **Corrected rule.** Skip type-2 transactions whose `provider-org/@ref` ends in `-COA-GOV` (including `Z1-COA-GOV`). These are counterpart funds, always with `ref="1"` and no loan number. The rule reproduces MapAfrica's `total_commitments_nongov`. Effects:
  - AfDB $100.3bn → $80.0bn
  - Total $283.8bn → $263.6bn
  - AfDB disbursed 44% → 56%
  - Medupi 9,055.4 → 1,711.4 (still the largest AfDB record)
- **World Bank is unaffected by that rule.** Its type-2 providers are only 44001 IBRD, 44002 IDA and 44003. The fix belongs at fetch_sources.py:321-325. The `currency = v.get("currency") or currency` line is harmless today.
- **The World Bank problem is larger in dollar terms** (#7/#8): $46bn of A02 budget support and about $69bn of non-infrastructure share. The fix is either:
  - drop A01/A02 and weight by the infrastructure share of the DAC `percentage` attributes, or
  - keep the money and rewrite the "budget support is left out" sentence.
- **AfDB IATI quirk.** Disbursements sometimes exceed commitments (Medupi 115%, CM-FA0-003 163%), so treat disbursement shares as indicative.
- **Files.** Raw downloads are in FC/raw_banks (afdb/, wb/, wbapi/, pages/), derived data in FC/work_banks, scripts in FC/scripts_banks. The repo was not touched.
