Every headline figure on the site reproduces exactly from the pinned GEM releases; the problems below come from GEM's own data or from our filters and labels.

**WRONG and MISLEADING**
1. **Wafa–Mellitah Pipeline 1 (WRONG, high):** shown as 5,246 km; GEM's wiki gives 524.6 km. GEM's own file has a dropped decimal (its estimate field says 525.69). Our `_num()` parsing is correct.
2. **Total pipeline route (WRONG, high):** 115,012 km. With the three confirmed fixes (Wafa, AKK, Egbin) it is 109,197 km; adding the probable Medgaz fix gives 108,779 km; Wafa alone gives 110,290 km. Operating route falls from 74,623 to 69,494 km (69,076 with Medgaz).
3. **AKK (Trans Nigeria Phase 1) (WRONG, med):** shown as 1,300 km; GEM's wiki gives 614 km, and the other 686 km is already a separate record. "Under construction" should be about 5,136 km, not 5,822.
4. **Egbin Node–Alagbado (WRONG, low):** 447 km; the wiki gives 40 km. **Medgaz onshore (WRONG, probable, low):** 845 km; about 427 km, since Medgaz's onshore leg is 547 km in total.
5. **Medgaz "drawn along its whole route" (WRONG, med):** it never reaches Spain. GEM's "offshore" geometry is an overland line across Algeria. Transmed, Greenstream and Nigeria–Morocco do reach Europe, but Nigeria–Morocco is drawn from Ghana, not Nigeria (MISLEADING, low).
6. **The Gambia missing from power (WRONG, med):** GEM writes "The Gambia" and our country list doesn't. Corrected: 4,285 units, 55 countries, 2,906 projects, 1,091.1 GW, 592.1 GW pipeline in 1,473 units.
7. **"Approved" = GEM "pre-construction" (MISLEADING, med):** GEM defines it as still seeking approvals, land or financing, not a signed commitment. This covers 601 units / 267.5 GW, including the AMAN project, which was paused in 2025.
8. **ACWA wind farm, Egypt, 10 GW "under construction" (MISLEADING, med):** GEM's own sources show only an MoU and a land allocation. That is 10 of the 75.2 GW shown as under construction.
9. **Pipeline dates and releases (MISLEADING, low):** "data as of 12 Oct 2026" is the download date; the newest record updates are 2025-09-03 (gas) and 2026-03-19 (oil). GEM's oil-tracker page names March 2025 as its latest release, not "2026-07".
10. **Power release is a month behind (DRIFTED, low):** GEM published a September 2026 release before our 12 Oct fetch: 4,284 units, 1,093.1 GW. The fetcher reads only the first 1,000 bucket entries, so it can never see newer releases.

## Notes

**Method.** I downloaded each GEM file by its exact release key and hashed it, rather than taking the newest. I re-implemented the site's filters in `scripts_gem/*.py`: the African country list, the status mappings, the length fallback order `LengthMergedKm → LengthKnownKm → LengthEstimateKm` used by `_num()`, and the rows the build step drops. Every headline power and pipeline figure reproduces exactly from upstream. So the problems are either errors in GEM's own data (#1, #3, #5, #6, the Medgaz geometry in #7, the ACWA status in #10) or our filter and label choices (#9, #11–14). For the outliers I used GEM's own wiki pages, plus press coverage where a project's size or status was in doubt.

**Length scan.** I compared each segment's GEM length with the length of its drawn route. 33 of 324 segments differ by a factor above 2.5 or below 0.4 (full list in `scripts_gem/pipes.out`). Most have `RouteAccuracy: low`, where the drawn line is wrong rather than the length. Examples:
- DP3–DP4–Sabratha: drawn 751 km for 8.5 km.
- NC 41–Mellitah: drawn 795 km for 130 km.
- Zohr–Al Gamil: drawn 14 km for 210–216 km.
- African Renaissance: stated 614 km, drawn 2,466 km.

Those only change what the map draws, because the site's km totals use GEM's length field. The length errors that do change totals are Wafa (10× its drawn route, on a route GEM rates "high" accuracy), AKK, Egbin and the Medgaz onshore segment. Sirte–Ras Lanuf–Sidra (700 km, drawn 237 km) matches GEM's wiki ("700 km"), so I left it as GEM's figure.

**Bugs noticed in passing (repo is read-only; nothing fixed):**
1. **The power fetcher can't see new releases.** `gem_latest_csv()` (fetch_sources.py:114-132) reads one page of 1,000 bucket entries and never asks for the next. The listing says `IsTruncated=true`, and the first 1,000 entries are all 2026-08-v2 files (mostly map tiles). So it can never see 2026-08-v3 (26 Aug) or 2026-09 (24 Sep).
   - Even with a full listing, `sorted(keys)[-1]` sorts `2026-08/…` after `2026-08-v3/…` ('-' sorts before '/'), so a base release would beat its own revisions.
   - Fix: list folders with `delimiter=/`, follow continuation tokens, and sort by version.
   - `gem_latest_pipeline_geojson()` has the same one-page pattern and works today only because those folders are small.
2. **The Gambia is dropped from power (#11).** The pipeline fetcher adds "The Gambia" as an extra check (fetch_sources.py:613); `fetch_gem()` (:145) does not. Simplest fix: add "The Gambia" to `AFRICA_NAMES`.
3. **A length check for pipelines.** When `LengthKnownKm / LengthEstimateKm` is above 3 or below 1/3 (27 African segments), prefer the estimate or flag the segment. That would have caught Wafa (9.98×) and AKK (3.6×). It would also flag Medgaz offshore, where the estimate is the wrong one because the drawn line is wrong.
4. **Medgaz offshore geometry is wrong in GGIT 2025-11 (#7).** It is an overland line, so the map draws a "Beni Saf–Almería" pipeline across the Algerian Sahara. Worth reporting to GEM through the error-report form linked on its tracker page.
5. **Eight drawn segments that GEM says have no route.** Their `RouteType` is "Not mapped" or "Unavailable" yet they ship geometry (e.g. Bordj Ménail–Algiers, `RouteAccuracy: no route`). This inconsistency is on GEM's side.
6. **Pipeline "data as of" shows the download date (#12).** `meta.pipelines.fresh` is set to `None` at fetch_sources.py:659. Consider storing the release month or the newest `LastUpdated`.
7. **French and British territories.** The rail's country filter lists Mayotte (3), Réunion (4) and Saint Helena (1) from other layers. GEM's 59 power units there (about 1.4 GW) are excluded by `AFRICA_NAMES`. That fits a "member states" scope, but someone filtering by Réunion sees no power plants.

**Caveats.**
- The GEM wiki is live (the Wafa page was edited on 14 Oct 2026), so wiki values can be newer than the frozen tracker. For the length errors I counted as WRONG, the frozen tracker's own estimate field and its sister segments agree with the wiki, so later edits can't explain the gap.
- The AMAN "paused in 2025" point rests on the title of a reference cited on GEM's AMAN page; I did not open the article.
- "54% exact" uses GEM's `location-accuracy` field. I did not verify the prose "the rest sit near the reported town or district"; GEM's "approximate" can mean a district or a region centroid.
