## Notes

- **How to fix rows 1–2.** For funders, splitting on " / " alone gives the right list: the only Resources values that join two bodies with " and " are "… and other resources" and "… and private sector resources", which can be mapped by hand. For implementers no regex works. The portal joins bodies with commas, which also appear inside single names ("Government of Côte d'Ivoire, Ministry of Finance", "Africa50 PD Manager, fund manager"), and uses `<br/>` as a line wrap. The options are a hand-kept alias table that counts each body once per project (giving the list in row 2) or dropping the line. The tally also runs on a field already cut to 160 characters (build_dashboard.py:635), which can cut a body name in half.
- **Rows 4–5** move the headline only to €4.39bn / $4.92bn, and the dashboard's rounded "€4.4bn" stays right. If TANIT KT is added, the €4.35bn literal in llms.txt and the methodology must change too.
- **NEDA (Côte d'Ivoire):** the portal itself is inconsistent (Amount €150m vs "up to EUR 100 million" in its text). This is not the site's error, but it is worth a footnote if the card is ever annotated. ERAVCDP is similar: it prints €17m while its text says "USD 20 million from the RPFF", which fits at roughly today's rate.
- **Benban:** the portal places Abydos II (AMEA Power, 1 GW PV + 600 MWh BESS, €110m Plafond Africa) "near Benban, in the Aswan Governorate". "The Benban solar park" (map.template.html:2735) also names the separate 1.65 GW Benban complex completed in 2019; "the Abydos II solar plant near Benban" is more exact.
- **"Directive" vs "pillar":** governo.it's English uses "pillars". "Directive" is a literal rendering of the Italian "direttrici" and reads in English as an order. Low priority.
- **BOAD and the pan-African renewables centre** are multi-country records drawn on a single named site (Lomé; Ben Guerir). That is why rows 3 and 26 differ from a pure country count.
- **No drift:** on 14 Oct 2026 the portal matches the 12 Oct extract on records, stages, pillars and amounts. Every difference above comes from parsing or wording.
- **Files:** raw pages, lists, legal notes, the speech and the ECB/Nominatim responses are in `FC/raw_mattei/`. Scripts (`parse.py`, `stats.py`, `money.py`, `tally.py`, `places.py`) are in `FC/scripts_mattei/`. Nothing in the repo was modified.
