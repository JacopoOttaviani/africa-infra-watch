- `checks.py` runs the netting arithmetic and the deflator ratio for each year.

## Notes
- **Headline figures.** Every headline count and sum on the 2,324 published records reproduces exactly from the frozen workbook. The faults are in the prose (rows 1, 3, 4, 5), an undisclosed drop of 26 records (row 6), and small undercounts from payload truncation (rows 7, 8).
- **RFA field.** The site never reads `Recommended For Aggregates`. Its filters equal RFA except that it keeps the Suspended and Cancelled records on purpose (−$8.56bn if removed). The Angola master-facility records are not double counted: AidData records drawn-down or net amounts precisely so they can be aggregated (row 10).
- **Upstream has moved on.** GCDF 3.0 has been superseded by China's Global Loans and Grants Dataset v1.0 (18 Nov 2025; to 2023; constant 2023 USD), and the card links now land on it. Some of the largest records have been split per lender, re-sized or recoded as cancelled. The "baseline" framing still holds, but "check it against the source" now fails for some records.
- **Deflation.** The China-specific deflator makes 2000–2006 commitments 2.0–2.6× nominal, about +25% overall.
- **Read-only.** No repo file was modified. Working files are in `FC/raw_china/` and `FC/scripts_china/`.
