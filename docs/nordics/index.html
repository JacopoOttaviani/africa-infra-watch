Lender profile · Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden

Nordic countries

Five providers, read as one: Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden. 18,216 activities with an African recipient for 2018–2024, USD 19.75 bn committed, all of it grants (0 loans); by provider, Sweden USD 7,896 m, Norway USD 6,725 m, Denmark USD 3,605 m, Finland USD 1,451 m, Iceland USD 72 m. The largest purpose is Material relief assistance and services, USD 1,678 m; Uganda, Somalia and Ethiopia receive the most.

The Nordic footprint on the ground is one company's: Scatec, the Norwegian developer, owns 65 of the power units with a Nordic owner in the map's layer, solar and hydro from Egypt's Benban to South Africa, Rwanda and Mozambique, with Norfund, Finnfund, KLP and Denmark's Frontier Energy fund beside it, and Equinor in two pipeline segments. Direct-investment stock is USD 6.6 bn where the five report it; Norway and Sweden publish no African partner. What African governments owe the five is USD 224 m at the end of 2024.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

Each of the five reports to the OECD at activity level: Danida, the Finnish foreign ministry, Iceland's agency, Norad and the Norwegian foreign ministry, Sida. Together they give 9,163 distinct titles for 18,216 records, grants to governments, multilateral trust funds and NGOs, with climate, energy access, governance and humanitarian aid the recurring purposes. Norway's and Sweden's records are the largest.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (7,228 records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

How to read this page

One record is
In the aid tables, one CRS activity as each of the five reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, immediate counterpart, summed over the reporters that publish African partners. Debt is the World Bank's bilateral public and publicly guaranteed stock. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • Norway's and Sweden's investment stocks by African country are not published at the OECD's level; the national statistics publish Africa as a region.
  • Norfund's, Swedfund's, IFU's and Finnfund's portfolios are published as company lists with amounts per investment on their own sites; the site does not read them yet. Norfund's is the largest and would add Globeleq's plants.
  • The five providers' IATI files carry locations for many projects; the site does not read them yet.
  • Submarine cables: the map's cable layer names no Nordic owner on an African landing.
