Lender profile · Kingdom of Spain

Spain

Spain's record is small money in many hands. 12,494 activities with an African recipient for 2018–2024, USD 2.11 bn committed, 100% grants and only 12 loans; 40% of the activities are reported by the Municipalities, the rest by the autonomous communities, the cities, the universities and the ministries. The largest purpose is COVID-19 control, USD 212 m; Morocco, Senegal and Mali receive the most, the Maghreb and the Sahel first.

The capital is across the strait. Spanish direct-investment stock in Africa stood at USD 7.0 bn at the end of 2024, Morocco 44% of it; the map's own layers carry 5 pipelines with Naturgy, Enagás or Repsol among the owners, the Medgaz line from Algeria and the Maghreb–Europe line through Morocco among them, and 13 power units owned by Acciona, Sener, Elecnor, Abengoa or Cobra, mostly solar and wind in South Africa, Egypt and Morocco. What African governments owe Spain is USD 1,137 m at the end of 2024.

Aid activities: OECD Creditor Reporting System

Spain reports to the OECD at activity level, with 9,292 distinct titles for 12,494 records, most of them the small grants of the cooperation agency, the regions and the municipalities to NGOs and to Morocco's, Senegal's and Mali's institutions. The few loans are the state's FONPRODE instruments. The record is one of many small things rather than works.

The purpose codes are the reliable part. The descriptions often name a region or a town (2,185 records mention one), but the CRS carries no coordinates, so the aid is drawn at country points and kept off the project map.

How to read this page

One record is
In the aid tables, one CRS activity as Spain reports it. In the energy table, one GEM power unit, one pipeline (its segments counted) or one cable, as the map carries them. In the series, one country and one year.
Status
GEM and TeleGeography records use the map's own mapping. Aid activities have no status.
Money
CRS commitments and disbursements are in current US dollars as reported. Net ODA is current prices. FDI stock is the OECD's net position, all resident units, immediate counterpart. Debt is the World Bank's bilateral public and publicly guaranteed stock. None of these is a project value and none is added to the map's lenders.
Location
Aid and the series are drawn at the country's interior point. Pipelines and cables are the map's routes, thinned; power units carry GEM's coordinates.
Not here, and why
  • The cooperation agency's IATI files carry the agency's projects with locations; the site does not read them yet.
  • CESCE export-credit cover and COFIDES's investments have no project register; where they show up it is through the CRS loan records.
  • Oil and gas fields with Spanish operators are in GEM's extraction tracker, released through a request form only.
