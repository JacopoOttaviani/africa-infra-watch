What these figures count

Source
World Bank, International Debt Statistics, release of __IDS_UPDATED__: series DT.DOD.DPPG.CD (public and publicly guaranteed debt outstanding, current USD) by creditor, through the World Bank's API (source 6). The borrowing governments report it, loan by loan, to the Bank's Debtor Reporting System. Open under CC BY 4.0; refreshed every December.
What is counted
Long-term external debt owed or guaranteed by a government or a public body, at the end of each year. Not counted: private debt that no government guarantees, short-term debt, domestic debt, and arrears the IDS does not attribute to a creditor.
A creditor's country
A lending country stands for every lender resident there, public and private: “China” is China Eximbank, China Development Bank and the Chinese commercial banks together; “United Kingdom” includes London-booked bank loans. The official-bilateral series alone (DT.DOD.BLAT.CD) misses the commercial banks, which is why it is not used here.
Bondholders
One anonymous creditor in the IDS. Whoever holds a Eurobond, a pension fund in New York or a bank in Lagos, the World Bank cannot say.
Multiple lenders
Syndicated loans the World Bank books to several lenders at once. Shown inside “Other lenders”.
The IMF
Use of IMF credit is a separate series (DT.DOD.DIMF.CD) that also carries the SDR allocations of 2009 and 2021, which are reserve assets rather than loans. It is shown beside the creditors, never added to them.
Missing
__MISSING__ do not report to the Debtor Reporting System, so the World Bank publishes no creditor detail for them.
Reproduce
python3 fetch_debt.py asks the API once per country, api.worldbank.org/v2/sources/6/country/KEN/series/DT.DOD.DPPG.CD/counterpart-area/all/time/all, and checks that the creditors add up to the World Bank's own total before writing data/debt.json.
