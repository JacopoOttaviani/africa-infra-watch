Global Gateway in Africa

The European Commission lists __N__ Global Gateway flagship projects in Africa, in __N_COUNTRIES__ countries, and gives no amount and no stage for any of them. Global Gateway is the European Union's banner for the infrastructure, energy, digital, health and education projects that it, its member states and their development banks back abroad, "Team Europe" in the Commission's words; it aims to mobilise up to EUR 300 bn. The flagships are the projects the Commission picks to show for it. Its own map gives each one an identifier, its countries, an area of partnership and a page, and most pages name the Team Europe members involved and a few milestones. That list is drawn here.

Climate and energy leads, with __TOP_AREA_N__ of the __N__ flagships (__TOP_AREA_PCT__%); __TOP_CTRY__ are named most. The money is elsewhere and not by project: the Commission's tracker says EUR __TRACKER_SSA__ bn was "mobilised" in Sub-Saharan Africa over 2021–2024, with no project, country or year behind the figure. The EU institutions' own records on the map pair by hand with __N_MATCHED__ of the flagships: EUR __MATCHED_EC_EUR_M__ m of the Commission's grants and contracts and EUR __MATCHED_EIB_EUR_M__ m signed by the EIB, kept apart, never added together and never a flagship's cost.

Where the flagships are

Each flagship is drawn at every African country it names, at that country's interior point. The Commission's map has one point per country and nothing finer, so the dots are spread around the point to be pointed at, not placed: a dot is a flagship in a country, not a site. Regional flagships are rings, drawn once in each country they name; __N_NOCTRY__ name no country and sit at the Commission's Africa-wide point in Chad, the one the map's EU layer uses for regional programmes.

What the money records say

There are two sets of figures for Global Gateway, and only one of them reaches a flagship. The Commission's tracker counts EUR __TRACKER_TOTAL__ bn "mobilised" worldwide over 2021–2024, EUR __TRACKER_SSA__ bn (__TRACKER_SSA_PCT__%) of it in Sub-Saharan Africa, about USD __TRACKER_SSA_USD__ bn. North Africa sits in a line with the Middle East and Türkiye (EUR __TRACKER_MENA__ bn), so the tracker gives no African total. The tracker names no project, country or year, and "mobilised" counts the member states' banks, the EIB and the EBRD, and, by the strategy's own aim, private money, so it is not what the Union spent.

The second set is the EU institutions' own: the Commission's contracts and financing decisions and the European Investment Bank's operations, which each publishes to IATI and which are the map's EU finance layer. The IATI files carry no flagship identifier, so a flagship and a record are paired here only where the record's title, or the decision above it, plainly names the same works, plant, programme or corridor, checked by hand. __N_MATCHED__ flagships pair with at least one record. __N_MATCHED_EC__ pair with __MATCHED_EC_RECS__ Commission records: EUR __MATCHED_EC_EUR_M__ m committed, and __MATCHED_EC_NOAMOUNT__ records still in preparation with no amount. __N_MATCHED_EIB__ pair with __MATCHED_EIB_RECS__ EIB operations: EUR __MATCHED_EIB_EUR_M__ m signed, and __MATCHED_EIB_NOAMOUNT__ cancelled or suspended and not counted; __MATCHED_BOTH__ records of both. The two institutions' figures are shown side by side and never added: a Commission grant and an EIB loan to the same works are different money, and each is the institution's own share. __MATCHED_OLD__ Neither covers the member states' development banks that many of the pages name, and only the sectors the layer keeps are searched (transport, energy, communications, water, industry and trade), so a health or education flagship's contracts are mostly outside it. NaturAfrica, the Commission's conservation programme, is the largest Commission pairing because the programme name is on every one of its contracts.

How to read this page

One record is
One flagship: one identifier on the Commission's Global Gateway map (a few flagships carry a list of identifiers and are one record). A flagship is African when the map lists it under an African country or its Sub-Saharan Africa point, or under its Mediterranean point (which mixes North Africa with the Middle East) when its page names an African country or North Africa. Regional flagships appear on the Commission's map under each country they name; here they are one record.
Location
The country's interior point, the site's usual point for a country, for every African country a flagship names; the Commission's map has one point per country too. A flagship that names no country sits at the Commission's Africa-wide point in Chad, as the map's EU layer places regional programmes. The data file, and the main map's Global Gateway layer, keep one point per flagship: the country's for a one-country flagship, the Africa-wide point otherwise.
Status
None. The Commission publishes no stage for a flagship, and the milestones on a page are undated sentences. No stage is inferred from them, and none that other trackers assign is used.
Money
None per flagship: the Commission publishes no amount for any of them. The tracker's regional figure and the EU layer's records are shown beside the list and never added to each other. The list's last two columns are what the Commission committed and what the EIB signed in the records that name the same works, in euros as published, each on its own and never added; neither is the flagship's cost or the Union's whole share.
Not here, and why
  • Global Gateway projects that are not flagships. The Commission says the list is not exhaustive.
  • EFSD+ guarantees, the main Global Gateway instrument, have no commitment transaction in the IATI files and leave no record on the map.
  • Amounts and stages per flagship, which the Commission does not publish.
