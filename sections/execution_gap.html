__MATRIX__

A unit counts in every list its owner string matches (__N_MULTI__ units match two), so the rows do not add up to the tracker. The lists are regular expressions on company names, written for the lender profiles, not an ownership register: they miss __MISSES__, and “__FP_NAME__” is read as American through “General Electric” (__FP_GW__ GW). Global Energy Monitor's Global Energy Ownership Tracker, which resolves each owner to a parent and a country, is the way to close the gap.

How to read this page

Sources
Global Energy Monitor, Global Integrated Power Tracker, release __GEM_RELEASE__ published __GEM_FRESH__, fetched __GEM_FETCHED__ by fetch_sources.py gem: every power unit in Africa, with its status, technology, capacity and owner as GEM files them (CC BY 4.0). AidData, __AD_RELEASE__, __AD_FRESH__, fetched __AD_FETCHED__ by fetch_sources.py china: the infrastructure commitments of Chinese official lenders and donors to African recipients, with commitment and completion years, funder, sector and flow (ODC-By 1.0; the locations the site draws come from GCDF 3.0 and its OpenStreetMap footprints, ODbL, and are not used here). Both sources allow commercial use with attribution.
Stage
GEM's own statuses are folded into the site's five, by GEM_STATUS in fetch_sources.py; this page prints the result and changes nothing:
    __GEM_FOLD__
“Retired” units count as operating by that rule and “stalled” mixes a cancelled project with a shelved one; GEM's status is on every unit's page.
Capacity
Each unit's capacity_mw, summed. GEM files the hydrogen schemes' later phases as “future phases” units of tens of gigawatts (the largest, __H2_BIGGEST__); they are counted as announced, which is what they are, and tagged H₂ so the reader can see them. Nothing is weighted by ownership share or probability.
Technology
GEM's tech field (its technology type, or the fuel where the tracker has no type) folded into ten groups: PV and assumed PV are solar; onshore, offshore and the wind tracker's “Unknown” mount type (__N_WIND_PH__ units, every one a wind farm by name, checked at build time) are wind; gas turbines, combined cycles, steam turbines, engines and the integrated solar combined cycles are gas and oil; subcritical to ultra-supercritical are coal; and so on. __N_RULE__ units carry no technology at all; __N_RULE_SORTED__ of them are sorted by a word in their name (nuclear, hydroelectric, coal, wind, solar, geothermal, gas, oil) and the other __N_NOT_STATED__, __GW_NOT_STATED__ GW, mostly a “power station” with no fuel, stay “not stated”.
Owners
The owner string GEM gives each unit, matched against the lender profiles' owner lists in fetch_donors.py (CHINESE_OWNER, GULF_OWNER, …): regular expressions on company names, written to find each lender's assets, not to classify the tracker. They resolve __N_RESOLVED__ of __N_UNITS__ units. A unit matching two lists counts in both (__N_MULTI__ units); the first match colours it in the solar chart, in the order China, Gulf, France, Nordics, Italy, Germany, Spain, Türkiye, United States, Japan. In the solar chart the European lists are one colour. Known misses: __MISSES__; “__FP_NAME__” matches the American list.
Hydrogen
A unit is tagged H₂ when its name or owner matches a pattern of the known green-hydrogen schemes (hydrogen, Megaton Moon, AMAN, Conjuncta, Project Nour, Hyphen, Zhero, NAYRAH, White Dunes, Tsau). The matched projects are listed in the page's data file so the tag can be checked.
The lag
AidData's commitment_year and completion_year, the difference in whole years; a record enters only with both. Quartiles by the inclusive method. The sector is AidData's, folded to the site's buckets by fetch_sources.py; the funder is the first institution AidData names, grouped as China Eximbank, the Ministry of Commerce, China Development Bank, the state commercial banks (ICBC, Bank of China, CCB, CITIC), the embassy, and “unspecified government body” where AidData could not name one. The flow is AidData's loan or grant. Because only finished projects have a completion year, the distribution is of what got done, not of what was promised; a project that never finishes has no lag.
What is not here
The AfDB, World Bank and EU finance layers on the site carry no dates, so nothing on this page uses them for speed. OpenStreetMap's construction works are not joined to GEM's units. Hypothesis 3 of the survey, that stated start dates slip, needs the next GEM release diffed against this one and is not tested. The owner lists resolve a tenth of the units; the full owner view needs GEM's Global Energy Ownership Tracker.
